Research Architecture

Frequently Asked Questions

How does QKV-LoRA adapt DINOv2 without full fine-tuning?
+
Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) freezes the pre-trained 86M parameter DINOv2 ViT-B/14 weights and injects trainable rank decomposition matrices into the query, key, and value ($Q, K, V$) projections of each multi-head self-attention block ($r=8, \alpha=16$). This allows the model to learn task-specific medical MRI features by updating only 822,020 parameters (0.95%), completely preserving the generalized spatial priors while adapting representation geometry.
How was data leakage and duplicate scans audited?
+
Prior to model lock, an exhaustive multi-phase deduplication audit evaluated all 7,200 raw scans using perceptual hashing (pHash, dHash, aHash) and structural similarity (SSIM > 0.95). A total of 459 duplicate/near-duplicate images across 371 connected component groups were identified and quarantined. The final cleaned dataset of 6,741 images was partitioned into 4,575 train, 809 validation, and a strictly isolated 1,357-image test cohort evaluated only once post-certification.
Why does LoRA significantly reduce Glioma-Meningioma diagnostic overlap?
+
In frozen foundation backbones, Gliomas and Meningiomas frequently share overlapping intensity profiles and edema patterns. By adapting the self-attention projections, LoRA learns to attend specifically to dural tail attachments (characteristic of extra-axial meningiomas) and infiltrating margin transitions (characteristic of intra-axial gliomas), reducing mutual cross-class errors from 53 down to 31 (a 41.5% reduction).
Can this platform execute completely offline on a local MacBook?
+
Yes. The entire platform runs locally on macOS with PyTorch Metal Performance Shaders (MPS) hardware acceleration. Pretrained DINOv2 weights and certified checkpoints are cached on local disk, requiring zero internet connectivity and achieving a warm inference latency of ~27.9 ms per scan.
Is this system approved as a clinical medical device?
+
No. This platform is an investigational deep learning research tool developed for scientific research, academic review, and algorithmic benchmarking. It is not approved by regulatory bodies (e.g. FDA, CE) for clinical diagnostic use. All outputs represent statistical probability distributions and must be verified by certified radiologists.
